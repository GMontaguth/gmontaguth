Research

Galaxies do not evolve alone. In groups and clusters they collide, merge, lose gas and get stripped, and these encounters change both how they look and whether they keep forming stars. I use multi-band imaging surveys to measure the structure of thousands of galaxies and ask which environments transform them, and how fast.

Galaxies in groups and compact groups

My PhD (University of La Serena, Chile) followed galaxies in compact groups from a multi-wavelength view, revealing a population of transition galaxies shaped by their environment and by the large-scale structures around them.

Groups, and groups falling into clusters

My current work follows galaxies in groups: how their structure and their stellar populations respond differently to the group environment, and how groups embedded within five virial radii of clusters pre-process their galaxies before they reach the cluster.

Multi-band surveys: S-PLUS and Rubin/LSST

I am a science member of S-PLUS, a member of the LSST Galaxies Science Collaboration and of the CHANCES (4MOST) morphology working group, and I develop tools for structural fitting in large surveys. I am now measuring the mass–size relation of galaxies with Rubin/LSST imaging.

In the news

AAS Nova, 4 May 2026

Featured Image: Galaxies Caught in Transition

The American Astronomical Society highlighted our study of about 4,000 galaxies in groups and in the field, which shows that transition galaxies are strongly shaped by the group environment.

Revista Pesquisa FAPESP, 4 June 2026 (in Portuguese)

Ambiente cósmico molda estrutura das galáxias de transição, que estão parando de gerar novas estrelas

FAPESP's magazine covered the same work: in groups, transition galaxies are smaller and more compact than in isolation, as if they had lost their outer discs.

Software & teaching

  • MorphoPLUS: multi-band structural fitting of galaxies in S-PLUS images (Python, bash, GalfitM). Used in my papers and by students I mentor.
  • S-PLUS SExtractor workshop: hands-on material for building catalogues from S-PLUS images of the Hydra cluster. Taught at the S-PLUS school From Image to Science, IAG/USP, July 2026.
  • Virtual Observatory class: finding and accessing astronomical data with VO tools.

I supervise and mentor undergraduate and graduate students at USP and the University of La Serena on galaxy structure, environment and machine-learning validation of structural fits.
