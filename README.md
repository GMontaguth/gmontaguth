## 🌌 About Me

I'm an astronomer from Colombia 🇨🇴 who studies **how galaxies change depending on where they live**. I'm currently a **postdoctoral researcher at the Universidade de São Paulo (Brazil)**, where I study **galaxy groups** and how these environments shape the evolution of the galaxies within them.

- 🏛️ **Now:** Postdoc at Universidade de São Paulo (USP), Brazil — galaxy groups
- 🎓 **PhD:** Universidad de La Serena (Chile) — galaxy evolution in **compact groups**: how dense environments drive morphological and physical transformations
- 🔭 **Research interests:** galaxy groups, galaxy formation & evolution, environmental effects, galaxy morphology, large-scale structure
- 🛰️ **Surveys & data I work with:** S-PLUS, LSST / Rubin, 4MOST, Virtual Observatory tools
- 🧩 **Methods:** multi-band photometry, source extraction, bulge–disc decomposition, morphological fitting
- 🎓 **Background:** BSc in Astronomy, Universidad de Antioquia (Colombia) — thesis on star formation and ISM ionization in minor galaxy mergers
- 🎤 **Outside research:** science comedy with *Quimikomedia*, podcasts, outreach talks… and running & hybrid training 🏃‍♀️
